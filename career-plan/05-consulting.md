# Consulting — the 6-Week Fit-Test

[← Index](INDEX.md)

**What this is:** a short, time-boxed experiment (weeks 1–6, ~2 hrs/week) to answer one question — *is consulting worth prepping for, yes or no?* — and then get out of the way. It ends in an explicit **keep-or-drop decision in Week 6**.

**Why it's boxed:** Consulting is your **#2-ranked direction**, behind Program/Project Management. It is being *tested, not pursued* — so it can never quietly eat the hours that belong to PM. The test is deliberately cheap: reading you'd enjoy anyway, conversations that fold into your [networking](03-networking.md) blocks, and one practice case with a classmate. If the answer is no, you reclaim the hours. If yes, you add *light* case prep in November — after the portfolio is built.

> **The honest framing:** you said the pull is **problem variety + research + strategy**. That *is* what consulting is. So this test is well-aimed — you're checking a real signal, not a fantasy. Either verdict is a win: a clean "no" is as valuable as a "yes" because it removes a distraction.

## The test — four cheap inputs

| # | Input | What it costs | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Read / watch** | ~1 hr/wk, wks 1–5 | Does the *shape* of the work interest you? |
| 2 | **Talk** (2–3 consultants) | folded into networking chats | What's the job *actually* like day-to-day? |
| 3 | **Taste** (1 practice case) | ~1 hr, wk 4 | Does the problem-solving feel *fun* or *forced*? |
| 4 | **Decide** (Week 6) | 15 min | Keep or drop — on paper, no waffling |

### 1. Read / watch
- **Pick ONE book** (don't read both): *Case Interview Secrets* — Victor Cosentino **OR** *Case in Point* — Marc Cosentino. See [Reading](04-reading-list.md). Cosentino's *Case Interview Secrets* is the gentler start.
  - You don't need to *finish* it. Read Ch 1–2 first (wk 2), keep going in wk 5 only if it's holding your interest. Losing interest **is data.**
- **Watch 2 "day in the life of a consultant" videos** (wk 1). Search McKinsey / BCG / Bain analyst day-in-the-life on YouTube. Notice your gut reaction to the travel, the pace, the client work.
- **One McKinsey or BCG problem-solving explainer** (wk 1–2). Search "McKinsey 7-step problem solving" or watch a BCG case walkthrough. This pairs perfectly with *Bulletproof Problem Solving* (your Week 1–4 book) — same muscle.

### 2. Talk — 2–3 consultants (zero extra load)
These are **not** new meetings. Aim 2–3 of your existing weekly [coffee chats](03-networking.md) at people who consult (or used to). Ask them the questions that reading can't answer:
- What does a normal Tuesday actually look like?
- What do you love about it — and what made people you know leave?
- For someone with a risk/compliance + ops background, is there a natural entry lane (e.g. ops/risk-focused consulting)?
- If you had my profile, would you do it again?

### 3. Taste — 1 practice case (wk 4)
- Do **one** practice case out loud with a classmate (many MSCM cohorts have consulting-curious peers — great networking too).
- Use a case straight from your chosen book. You're not being graded. You're checking one thing: **did structuring the messy problem feel energizing or draining?**

## ⭐ Week-6 Decision Framework

Answer these honestly. There are no wrong answers — a clean "no" frees real hours for PM.

- [ ] **The core question:** Does the work — *problem variety, research, structuring strategy* — excite me enough that I'd *want* to spend November evenings prepping cases?
- [ ] **The videos/reading:** Did the day-in-the-life pull me in, or make me tired?
- [ ] **The people:** Did the consultants I spoke to describe a life I want?
- [ ] **The case:** Did the practice case feel like *play* or like *homework*?
- [ ] **The opportunity cost:** Every hour of Nov case prep is an hour *not* spent on PM stories, mocks, and referrals. Is consulting worth that trade?

**Score it simply:** mostly yeses → **KEEP**. Mixed or mostly noes → **DROP**. Don't overthink; trust the gut signal the test was built to surface.

| Verdict | What happens next |
|---|---|
| ✅ **KEEP** | Add *light* case prep to November ([Polish phase](MASTER-SCHEDULE.md)). PM stays #1 — consulting rides alongside, it does not take over. Revisit [Reading](04-reading-list.md) for a case-prep cadence. |
| ❌ **DROP** | Reclaim the ~2 hrs/wk. Pour them into PM: [Projects](02-projects.md), [Networking](03-networking.md), and story-building. No guilt — you *tested* it, you didn't guess. |

> Whichever way it goes, consulting stops being a background "what if" question and becomes a settled decision. That clarity is the real deliverable.
