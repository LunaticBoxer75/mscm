# Reading List

[← Index](INDEX.md)

You read about **one book a month**, so this is built to be *browsable*, not a marathon. Four priority books are already slotted into your Build weeks (Section A). Everything else is an à-la-carte menu — grab one when you have energy, skip guilt-free when you don't.

**How to read for a job search:** read for the **one idea you can use this week**, not for completion. If a book gives you one framework you can name in an interview or apply to a project, it did its job — put it down and move on.

## Section A — ⭐ Priority 4 (read first, scheduled across Build)

One per month, each paired with the projects and skills running the same weeks. Read these in order.

| Book | Author | Skill it builds | Why HER specifically | Scheduled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bulletproof Problem Solving** | Conn & McLean | Structured problem-solving — issue trees, hypotheses, "define the real problem before solving it" | This *is* your edge, turned into a repeatable, nameable method. Turns "I'm good at framing problems" into "I use the 7-step problem-solving process." Also fuels your [consulting](05-consulting.md) fit-test running the same weeks. | **Wks 1–4** |
| **The Goal** | Goldratt | Theory of Constraints — bottleneck thinking, throughput, operations flow | It's the exact mental model behind your **Starbucks throughput flagship** (wks 5–8, same window): find the bottleneck, fix the constraint. Written as a novel — an easy read that fits the August deload. Core to the Ops / Supply Chain goal. | **Wks 5–8** |
| **The Pyramid Principle** | Minto | Top-down executive communication — lead with the answer, structure the "why" | Communication shows up in **74%** of PM job descriptions. Your differentiator is the *recommendation* at the end of each case study — this teaches you to package it so leaders act on it. Your Polish-phase STAR answers are meant to be Pyramid-structured. | **Wks 9–12** |
| **Financial Intelligence** | Berman & Knight | Business & financial literacy — read a P&L, talk cost, ROI, and budget | Your brand line is "better *and cheaper*." Budget appears in **42%** of PM JDs. This lets you speak cost-savings and ROI credibly in interviews — the language decision-makers respect. | **Wks 13–16** |

No schedule. Pick by which goal you want to feed that month.
