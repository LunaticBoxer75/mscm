## 6. Track 2 — Portfolio (makes the brand *provable*)

**Structure:** 3 flagship (deep, full case study, interview centerpieces) + 7 light (vibe-coded / analysis / strategic memo). Target: 10 total.

**Sourcing method (the reusable skill):**
1. **Public pain points** — earnings calls, Glassdoor, App Store/Play reviews, Reddit, news ("Starbucks mobile-order bottleneck").
2. **Public data** — Kaggle, 10-Ks, CFPB complaints DB, Yelp/App reviews, Inside Airbnb, gov/health datasets.
3. **The JD mirror** — reverse-engineer projects from what target JDs need solved.
4. **Her own scar tissue** — Uber problems she lived (appeals queue, fraud triage, SOP gaps), recreated with public/synthetic data.

**Flagship case-study format (consistent across all):**
> **Problem** (1–2 sentences, real & public) → **Approach** → **Key finding** (with a number) → **What I built** (SQL/BI/Python) → **Recommendation** *(her insight/strategy layer — the differentiator)* → **What I'd do next.**

**Portfolio home:** one clean Notion page (or simple site) — About → 3 flagships with big impact numbers → 7 light projects in a grid → resume + LinkedIn links. **Every project ends in a recommendation**, because her edge is the strategy, not the query.

## 7. Track 3 — Consulting fit-test (time-boxed → decision)

Runs **weeks 1–6, ~2 hrs/week**, then a **verdict**. Never competes with PM.
- **Read/watch:** *Case Interview Secrets* (Cosentino) or *Case in Point* + a few "day in the life of a consultant" videos + one McKinsey/BCG problem-solving explainer.
- **Talk:** 2–3 conversations with real consultants (folds into networking — no extra load).
- **Taste:** one practice case with a classmate.
- **Week-6 decision:** *Does the work (variety, research, strategy) excite me enough to prep cases?* → **keep** (add light case prep in Nov) or **drop** (reclaim hours for PM). Either answer is a win.
