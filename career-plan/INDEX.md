## How to use this binder

- **Print ONE weekly page each Sunday night.** That single sheet is your whole week — day-by-day, what to study, which project, who to reach out to. Don't print the reference pages.
- **The reference pages are browsable, not printed.** During the week, dip into them when you need something: grab an outreach message from [Networking](03-networking.md), pull the next book from [Reading](04-reading-list.md), pick your next project from [Projects](02-projects.md), check a skill's depth bar from [Skills](01-skills.md).
- **The weekly page tells you WHEN; the reference pages tell you HOW.** If a week says "send alumni msg #2," the exact template lives in Networking.
- **Sunday, 15 min:** run the weekly review at the bottom of the page, then print next week's sheet. That's the whole ritual.
- **Tech skills are for proof, not filters.** Your risk/compliance/stakeholder background already clears keyword filters — SQL/Python exist so your portfolio *shows* the strategy and you can say "I built this" in the room. Depth bar: *fluent, can build a prototype, make a storyline — not an engineer.*
