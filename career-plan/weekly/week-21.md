# Week 21 · Nov 30 – Dec 6 · MARKET — "Hit the market"

[← Index](../INDEX.md)

> 🎯 **This week's mission:** Go live. Send **5 tailored applications** to dream + target companies, and turn every chat into a "flag me openings" ask. Build is done, portfolio is live — now the portfolio does the talking.

The skills grind is over. From here, your free blocks are **apply + prep** blocks. Quality over volume: 5 sharp, tailored apps beat 20 sprayed ones.

## Day-by-day

| Day | Status | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 🎓 Class 3–9pm (light only) | Before class: send **1 networking DM** asking a contact to flag openings. Skim [find-jobs](../CLAUDE.md) fresh listings 15 min; bookmark 5–8 roles. |
| **Tue** | ✅ Free (cap 3–4 hrs) | Run [find-jobs](../CLAUDE.md), shortlist the 5 best-fit roles (dream first). Tailor + **submit apps #1–2** (resume variant + portfolio link). |
| **Wed** | 🎓 Class 6–9pm | 2–3 hr block before class: tailor + **submit app #3**. Draft 3 tight talking points for your top-choice role. |
| **Thu** | ✅ Free (cap 3–4 hrs) | **Submit apps #4–5.** **Coffee chat #1** — ask them directly to flag/refer openings at their company. |
| **Fri** | ✅ Free | **Coffee chat #2 / networking event.** **LinkedIn post** ("Portfolio's live — here's what I built, and I'm now looking for PM/ops roles"). |
| **Sat** | 🎓 Class 9–6 · night OFF 🔒 | No work after class. Rest — December is a sprint. |
| **Sun** | ✅ Free | Overflow: finish any of the 5 apps not yet in. **Prep for early responses** (portfolio walkthrough, top STAR answers). Assignment buffer. **Weekly review (15 min).** |

- **What to link in each app:** your live portfolio + 3 flagships — see [Projects](../02-projects.md).
